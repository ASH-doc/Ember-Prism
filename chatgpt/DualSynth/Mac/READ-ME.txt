DUAL SYNTH — MAC STANDALONE AND VST3

Apple Silicon arm64, macOS11+ target. Use Install-Mac.command or copy the
app to ~/Applications and the VST3 bundle to ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3.
Open standalone Options to choose the audio and MIDI device, or rescan
VST3 plugins in your DAW. The binaries are ad-hoc signed and not notarized.

The A/B tabs switch the editor. Both engines keep playing. Choose Layer,
Split, A only or B only in the routing panel. The master effects are
bypassed by default, and the mixer controls only the engine outputs.

The packaged VST3 passed a native host test. Desktop standalone launch
could not be verified in the build session. The companion complete archive
includes the corresponding C++ source, JUCE, licenses, Windows build files,
installation details and validation logs.
